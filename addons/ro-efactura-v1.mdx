Key: `ro-efactura-v1`

Module: [github.com/invopop/gobl.ro.anaf](https://github.com/invopop/gobl.ro.anaf)

Support for the Romanian national e-invoicing system,
RO e-Factura, operated by ANAF. The addon applies the
national rules (BR-RO) RO_CIUS adds to the EN 16931
semantic model: the document types ANAF files, the
ISO 3166-2:RO subdivisions and Bucharest sector codes
addresses have to carry, the VAT accounting currency,
the identifiers of the seller and the buyer, and the
length and count limits of every element.

Built on the EU EN 16931 CIUS profile, declared as a
dependency.

## Sources
- [Order MF 1366/2021 approving RO_CIUS and the national operational rules](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMF_1366_2021.pdf)
- [Order MF 4092/2022 amending RO_CIUS (version 1.0.1, BR-RO table)](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMF_4092_2022.pdf)
- [RO e-Factura technical information and schematron](https://mfinante.gov.ro/ro/web/efactura/informatii-tehnice)
